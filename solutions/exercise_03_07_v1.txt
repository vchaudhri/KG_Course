
Examine the schema in Wikidata

Pick one company. Say Apple. Query all its properties.


SELECT DISTINCT
  ?property
  ?propertyLabel
  ?propertyDescription
WHERE {
  wd:Q312 ?prop ?value .

  ?property wikibase:directClaim ?prop .

  SERVICE wikibase:label {
    bd:serviceParam wikibase:language "en".
  }
}
ORDER BY ?propertyLabel
